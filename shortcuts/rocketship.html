🚀 Rocketship Transformations — CS460

Raw WebGL2: move, rotate, and scale a rocketship with one matrix uniform.

Coordinate system: origin at canvas center; +X is right, +Y is up. The faint grid marks 0.1 world-unit intervals.

Transformation matrix

M = T × R × S for column vectors. The GLSL vertex shader applies u_matrix * vec3(a_position, 1.0).
